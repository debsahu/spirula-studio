Spirula Studio's densify host stage is a port of this plugin's host logic,
from commit ab0b04e35b12bff65ee87bdaacfa3177c21521d6. The ported files, and
what they came from:

  src/roma/Sample.{h,cpp}     core/sampling.py   (certainty sampling with coverage)
  src/roma/Select.{h,cpp}     core/selection.py  (reference and neighbour selection)
  src/roma/Densify.{h,cpp}    core/pipeline.py, core/geometry.py, densify.py
                              (collect, triangulation, filters, fusion, support,
                              track filter, voxel select, point cap)
  src/roma/DensifyRun.cpp     densify.py, core/pipeline.py (the reference loop:
                              which views are matched against which, masks
                              applied before matching, the run's sample budget)

The plugin's vendored RoMaV2 tree, its GUI panels and its COLMAP writer were not
ported. Spirula Studio is distributed under the GNU General Public License
version 3 (LICENSE at the repository root), which the plugin's
GPL-3.0-or-later permits. Where the port departs from the plugin, and the
--plugin-exact mode that reproduces it, is docs/notes/densify.md.
